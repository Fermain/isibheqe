Tessellation check sheet

Common isiZulu words, each drawn twice: as the ordinary line, and tessellated. The small letters under the tessellated form give the reading of each shape; a dot joins syllables that share a square (read left, top, bottom, right). For proofreading the experiment on the tessellation branch. This page is not part of the built site.

Things to look for: a word that falls apart into pieces; a cluster you cannot read back in order; marks that collide or become too small; a lone glyph that sits oddly beside a cluster.

Your ticks and notes are saved as you go to check-sheet.results.json beside the code, so they survive a reload and can be read back.
