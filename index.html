Every triangle is one syllable. Type below to write. Learn to read it in six steps.

Colour

Choose any enclosed part of a glyph to fill it. Colour is for art only and does not change what the text says. Choose the colour again to stop painting.

Punctuation is left out for now. Write m' or n' for a nasal that is a syllable by itself. For other languages, or to adjust a syllable by hand, .

Choose a vowel first. Consonant marks are drawn onto the vowel's triangle.

The small letters are keyboard shortcuts, the same ones as the original 2015 tool. Pressing a consonant mark again removes it.
